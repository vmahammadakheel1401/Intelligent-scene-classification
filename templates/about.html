AI Approach for Intelligent Scene Image Classification

How the model works, how well it does, and where the work came from.

How it works

  1. Prepare the photo. It is resized to {{ meta.img_size }} × {{ meta.img_size }} pixels and its colours are normalised the same way as during training.
  2. Look at it. A ResNet50 network, first trained on 1.2 million ImageNet photos, turns the image into 2,048 numbers that describe what it sees.
  3. Decide. A small classifier head (2048 → 512 → 128 → 6) turns those numbers into a probability for each of the six scenes. The highest one is the answer.

Limitations

  • It only knows six scenes. A photo of a desert, a room or a person still gets one of the six answers, which is why the page warns when confidence is low.
  • Glacier and mountain photos look alike and are the most common mix-up, as are street and buildings.
  • The training photos are small (150 px), so very detailed photos lose detail when they are shrunk.
